## Brand & Style
The design system for **13th Avenue** captures the high-energy, trend-setting digital presence of **PrettyLittleThing (PLT)** combined with the elevated luxury of a lifestyle brand house. 

The parent brand is minimalist, utilizing high-contrast charcoal/black and white grids. The core promotional identity is driven by **Hot Pink (#FF007F)**, capturing immediate attention, creating retail urgency, and identifying call-to-actions.

Sub-brands express their unique identities within this container:
*   **Miss Chloé (Fashion):** Clean editorial grids with refined typography, highlighting structured silhouettes, elegant abayas, and seasonal trends.
*   **Nella Hair Couture (Beauty):** Soft, gold-accented typography and close-up lifestyle imagery focusing on quality extensions and professional wigs.
*   **COCO (Lifestyle):** A balanced, structured grid layout displaying organic home details, children's essentials, and tech accessories.

## Colors
The color strategy mimics modern fast-luxury e-commerce:
*   **Brand Base:** Clean white (`#ffffff`) background with charcoal text (`#0f0f10`) provides readability and matches PLT's high-contrast grid lines.
*   **Conversion Pink:** **Hot Pink (#FF007F)** is our primary action color, used for promo announcements, "Add to Bag", and active shopping cart triggers.
*   **Luxury Gold:** **Champagne Gold (#C5A059)** acts as a sub-brand secondary accent for Miss Chloé and Nella Hair Couture labels.
*   **Sale Red:** Muted red (`#ef4444`) for markdown price labels and clearance indicators.

## Typography
We pair two Google Fonts to create the brand voice:
*   **Outfit:** A geometric sans-serif loaded for all functional elements, product listings, category cards, navigation, and body copy. It is clean, modern, and ensures fast scanning.
*   **Cormorant Garamond:** An elegant, high-contrast serif font used for brand logos, collection titles, and sub-brand headings to elevate the parent lifestyle label.

## Layout & Spacing
*   **Announcement Bar:** A persistent 36px tall hot pink bar at the absolute top drives urgent campaigns.
*   **Header Navigation:** A clean white header that becomes sticky on scroll. It contains the mega-menu directories, search input, and right-aligned icons (Search, Account, Wishlist, Cart Drawer trigger).
*   **Grids:** Layout grids use a fluid 12-column setup. For product grids, we use a 4-column layout on desktop, transitioning to a 2-column layout on mobile, keeping borders thin and minimal (1px light gray dividers).
