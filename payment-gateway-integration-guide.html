2. Merchant Registration & Credentials Needed
A. CCAvenue UAE (Credit & Debit Cards)
Direct Card Gateway
Processes Visa, MasterCard, and American Express in AED. Funds settle directly into your UAE corporate bank account.
Registration Portal: https://www.ccavenue.ae
Credential Name Description & Purpose
Merchant ID Your unique merchant account identifier assigned by CCAvenue UAE.
Access Code Public authorization key enabling the checkout form to initiate card charges.
Working Key (Secret) 256-bit encryption key used to securely sign and verify transactions.
Environment Confirmation of whether the keys belong to Staging (Test) or Live (Production).
B. Tamara (Buy Now, Pay Later - 3 or 4 Split Payments)
BNPL Solution
Allows shoppers to pay in 3 or 4 interest-free installments. Tamara pays you 100% of the purchase upfront, increasing conversion rates and average basket sizes.
Registration Portal: https://partners.tamara.co
Credential Name Description & Purpose
API Merchant Token Secure bearer token required for server-side order and capture authorization.
Notification Key / Webhook Verifies real-time payment success notifications from Tamara to our database.
Public Key Used to render dynamic installment banners on product and cart pages.
Account Currency & Country United Arab Emirates (AED) confirmation.
3. Implementation Roadmap (Our Commitment)
  1. Credential Setup: Once you submit the applications and receive your keys, share them securely with our development team.
  2. SDK & Widget Integration: We embed Tamara installment badges on product pages and connect the secure card form for CCAvenue.
  3. Sandbox Testing: We execute full test orders (success, refund, 3D Secure verification) to guarantee zero downtime.
  4. Live Production Launch: Immediate switch to live processing with real-time settlement tracking in your merchant dashboards.
